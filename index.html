Frequently Asked Questions

How do I earn gold faster?

Upgrade your buildings regularly and make sure you are unlocking new ones as soon as you can afford them. You can also watch a short optional ad to activate a 2x gold multiplier for 2 full hours.

What does prestige do?

Prestiging resets your current run but awards you powerful legacy bonuses that carry over permanently. Each prestige cycle makes your next run significantly stronger and faster.

Does the game earn gold while I am offline?

Yes. Your buildings keep generating gold even when the app is closed. Come back after a few hours and collect everything that has built up.

What does Remove Ads include?

Remove Ads is a one time purchase that removes all ads from the game. You will still receive the 2x gold bonus instantly any time you want it, without needing to watch anything.

How do I restore my Remove Ads purchase?

Tap the Restore Purchases button found beneath the Remove Ads option in the game. As long as you are signed into the same Apple ID you used to purchase, it will restore automatically.

I lost my progress. Can it be recovered?

Progress is saved locally on your device. If you have deleted and reinstalled the app, unfortunately local save data cannot be recovered. Please contact us if you believe this is a bug.

The app crashed or is not loading. What do I do?

Try closing and reopening the app first. If the issue continues, try deleting and reinstalling. If you are still experiencing problems please contact us with your device model and iOS version.

Contact Support

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Realm's Rise is developed by Jeremy. This privacy policy explains how your information is handled when you use the app.

Data We Collect

Realm's Rise does not directly collect any personal information. The app uses Google AdMob to serve optional rewarded ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data in accordance with Google's privacy policy. You will be asked for permission before any advertising identifier is accessed on iOS 14 and above.

In App Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple via the App Store. We do not collect or store any payment information.

Local Storage

Game progress is saved locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted to any server.

Children

Realm's Rise is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
